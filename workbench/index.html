        2. Train

        Add at least 4 examples of Paper, Plastic, and No item.

        Add examples, then train on this computer.

        Four drawings of paper, four of a bottle, and four empty tables. They are drawings, not photos. Use them when you do not have objects yet.

        What training does

        A small pre-trained model called MobileNet describes each image. Training then teaches a three-class layer from your examples. The first run downloads about 1.9 MB from this website. Later runs can reuse the browser’s copy. Cancel leaves the buttons usable.

        3. Test the sorter

        After training, try an object you did not train on, then try an empty frame. The flap should hold for No item, a score under 70%, a close call, or a feed that has stopped.
