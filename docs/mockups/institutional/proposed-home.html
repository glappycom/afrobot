Programs

A pathway for every age

Children start where they are and grow with us, from their first hands-on build to leading a competition team.

Ages 6–9

Discover

First builds by hand. Children learn how machines move and work in small teams.

Explore Discover
Ages 9–12

Build

Larger builds, circuits and first code. Teams carry a project from idea to working robot.

Explore Build
Ages 13–17

Innovate · Compete · Advance

AI-powered humanoid robots, competition seasons, and older students mentoring younger teams.
