# Global content strategy

Enhancement of the public site. The brand, forms, age divisions, and published 2027 season stay. New pages say what is operating, what was a pilot, and what is only proposed.

## Lines

Use these and do not add others.

- Lead line: Two Continents. One Innovation Future.
- One supporting line: Learn. Build. Compete. Innovate.
- Tagline, unchanged: From Africa to the World.

The lead line is the story heading on the home page. The supporting line appears once, under the home hero. The tagline stays on the hero mark and in the footer.

## Narrative

We Have Built (Cameroon programs and the McKinney pilot) → We Have Learned (age path and season rules already published) → We Are Scaling (proposed US timeline) → We Are Connecting (USA and Africa pages, pathway cards) → We Are Innovating (Innovation Lab, proposed challenge). The path is Academy → League → Global Innovation Challenge → Future Opportunities. Future Opportunities is a direction, not a requirement to start a company.

## Navigation

One row, nothing under More.

**Programs · USA · Africa · Impact · Partners**

Sponsor a Student stays the nav button. It is the existing primary action and it still fits.

Events and Volunteer move to the footer. They are real pages, and a sixth and seventh item would crowd the row under 1100px. About, Moonshot, Collaboration, and the Challenge are secondary for the same reason: the top row is the map of the organization, and those pages are reached from the footer and from in-page links.

Footer groups:

- Explore: Programs, USA, Africa, Impact
- The work: About, Moonshot, Collaboration, Challenge, Mentorship
- Contact: Partners, Events, Volunteer, Contact, info@afrobot.org

Mobile uses the same five links, then the secondary links as quiet items. No More label.

## Page hierarchy

- Home: hero, two-number impact strip, US–Africa cards, four-step path, published season block, audience rows, close.
- Programs: Academy, League, Innovation Lab, then the existing age divisions and cost goals.
- USA: proposed 2027–2028 timeline and regional cards, plus the published pilot rules and a link to competitions.
- Competitions: keep the published 2027 footprint and the 8-team pilot sentence.
- Africa: Cameroon as the operating foundation. No other country described as operating.
- Collaboration and Challenge: proposed only. No dates, awards, funding, or participant counts.
- Impact: founder-reported reach, with photos already on the site.
- Moonshot: the 10-times question as an ambition, with levers and evidence still to collect.
- Partners: opportunity types and partnerships@afrobot.org. No named partners.
- About: history, mission, footprint, philosophy, timeline. Unknown dates stay as code comments.
