A pathway for every age

From first builds at six to competition teams at seventeen, every stage has a coach, a team and real tools.

Planned sessions
Ages 6–9

Discover

First builds by hand. Children learn how machines move and work in small teams, including a life-size vehicle they build together.

Ages 9–12

Build

Larger builds, circuits and first code. Teams carry a project, including a life-size vehicle, from idea to a working build.

Ages 13–17

Innovate · Compete · Advance

An AI agent is the brain of a humanoid robot. Teams prepare those robots for recognized robotics competitions, and older students mentor younger teams with an adult coach.

Programs

Three programs

Academy

AfroBot Academy

Children learn to code and build with a mentor. Lessons are also at learn.afrobot.org.

League

AfroBot League

A team can start in one town, meet teams nearby, and then come together at a national event. A later meet between countries has no date yet.

Lab

AfroBot Innovation Lab

Children try a prototype for a real problem, such as water or farming. They do not have to start a company.
