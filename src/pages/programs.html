Programs

Three programs

Academy

AfroBot Academy

Kids learn to code and build with a mentor. Lessons are also at learn.afrobot.org.

League

AfroBot League

A team can start in one town, then meet teams nearby, then meet at a national event. A later meet between countries has no date yet.

Lab

AfroBot Innovation Lab

Kids try a prototype for a real problem, such as water or farming. They do not have to start a company.
