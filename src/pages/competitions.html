Competitions

Prepare for the stage. Know who runs it.

AfroBot aims to prepare students for established competitions such as FIRST Tech Challenge. AfroBot is not affiliated with FIRST.

Preparation is practice: building, testing, and learning how a public challenge works.

FIRST, and any other organizer, sets eligibility, fees, and calendar. Eligibility for Compete follows that organizer. Suggested ages on other AfroBot programs are guidance.

No competition calendar is confirmed.
