Competitions

Prepare for the stage. Know who runs it.

AfroBot aims to prepare students for established competitions such as FIRST Tech Challenge. AfroBot is not affiliated with FIRST.

Preparation means practice: building, testing, and learning how a public challenge works. It does not mean AfroBot enters a team, pays a competition fee, or holds a roster spot.

The interest list is not team placement. FIRST, and any other organizer, sets its own eligibility, fees, and calendar. Those rules are not AfroBot’s to rewrite, and they are not published here as if they were ours.

What we will not put on this page

  • A date that has not been confirmed.
  • A claim that a student has qualified, won, or been selected.
  • A partner mark, including any mark that belongs to a competition organizer.

Eligibility for Compete is set by the competition. Suggested ages on other AfroBot programs are only guidance.
