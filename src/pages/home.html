What happened in McKinney in 2024

A community build day was held in McKinney, Texas, in 2024. Kids and mentors posed with robots they had built. Three robots sit on the floor in front of the group.

The same day included an AI track: kids at tables with their robots, and a presenter at a screen. A short clip shows kids testing robots on a mission mat. The clip has no spoken narration.

Keeping the cost within reach

A family should not have to buy a whole lab before a child can make something. Equipment is meant to stay with the program. When funding exists, a sponsor can cover a place. Adults who help in person are screened before they work with students. A host may be supported by a grant. No sponsored place and no grant is listed on this site today.
