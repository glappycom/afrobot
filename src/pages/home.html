Keeping the cost within reach

A family should not have to buy a whole lab before a child can make something. Equipment is meant to stay with the program. When funding exists, a sponsor can cover a place. Adults who help in person are screened before they work with students. A host may be supported by a grant. No sponsored place and no grant is listed on this site today.

A goal, not a price. Our goal is an entry workshop around $10. That amount is a goal, not a current price. Nothing on this page is an offer to register, and no fee is being charged from this website.

Sessions are not open yet

Discover, Build, Innovate, Compete, and Advance are names for sessions we are preparing. There is no schedule, no price, and no registration. This one note covers all of them.

Suggested ages 5–8

Discover

Playful building and simple machines, with an adult close by.

Suggested ages 9–12

Build

A longer project: design something, make it, and test it with a team.

Suggested ages 12–15

Innovate

Harder problems, including how AI tools are used and how a team explains its choices.

Eligibility set by the competition

Compete

Practice toward competitions run by other organizations. AfroBot does not set their rules.

Suggested ages 15–18

Advance

Practice and guidance toward further learning. Not an application, and not a scholarship offer.
