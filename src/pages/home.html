What a child can do next

A child can take a class, join a team, and later try a bigger project. Starting a company is optional.

  1. Academy

    Classes with a mentor.

  2. League

    A local team.

  3. Global Innovation Challenge

    We plan a shared project for teams in different countries. No date yet.

  4. Future Opportunities

    More school, a job, or a project they choose.

The work

Two Continents. One Innovation Future.

Ages

A sponsor can cover the cost of a place.

  1. Discover

    Ages 6–9. Teams build a life-size vehicle by hand.

  2. Build

    Ages 9–12. Teams carry a life-size vehicle through a longer build.

  3. Innovate

    Ages 13–17. An AI agent is the brain of a humanoid robot.

  4. Compete

    Ages 13–17. Teams prepare humanoid robots for recognized robotics competitions.

  5. Advance

    Ages 13–17. Older students mentor younger teams with an adult coach.
