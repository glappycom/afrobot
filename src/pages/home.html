The path

The stages

Affordable and sponsored entry removes the cost barrier.

  1. Discover

    Ages 6–9. Teams build a life-size vehicle by hand.

  2. Build

    Ages 9–12. Teams carry a life-size vehicle through a longer build.

  3. Innovate

    Ages 13–17. An AI agent is the brain of a humanoid robot.

  4. Compete

    Ages 13–17. Teams prepare humanoid robots for recognized robotics competitions.

  5. Advance

    Ages 13–17. Older students mentor younger teams with an adult coach.
