What happened in McKinney in 2024

An AfroBot program was held in McKinney, Texas, in 2024. Kids and mentors posed with robots they had built. Three robots sit on the floor in front of the group.

The same day included an AI track: kids at tables with their robots, and a presenter at a screen. A short clip shows kids testing robots on a mission mat.

Keeping the cost within reach

A family should not have to buy a whole lab before a child can make something. Equipment stays with the program. When funding exists, a sponsor can cover a place. Adults who help in person are screened before they work with students.

$10

A goal, not a price. Our goal is an entry workshop around $10.

Sessions are not open yet

Discover, Build, Innovate, Compete, and Advance are names for sessions we are preparing.

Suggested ages 5–8

Discover

Playful building and simple machines, with an adult close by.

Suggested ages 9–12

Build

A longer project: design something, make it, and test it with a team.

Suggested ages 12–15

Innovate

Harder problems, including how AI tools are used and how a team explains its choices.

Eligibility set by the competition

Compete

Practice toward competitions run by other organizations.

Suggested ages 15–18

Advance

Practice and guidance toward further learning.
