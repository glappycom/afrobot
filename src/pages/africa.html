01

Why we started

Kids in Bamenda were in school about four days a week because of the conflict there. Kids in other parts of the country were in school five days a week.

We wanted Bamenda kids on equal footing. AI and robotics classes were a way to give them that edge. The classes began in {{facts.cameroonStarted}}, under Glappy Foundation.

02

The holiday program

The holiday program is how the work grew. Every holiday we open the doors, bring more kids into the school, and train them in AI and robotics.

Nearly {{facts.cameroonTrained}} children have trained this way. The number grows with each holiday.
