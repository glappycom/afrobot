Why we started

Kids in Bamenda were in school about four days a week because of the conflict there. Kids in other parts of the country were in school five days a week.

Why we started

Equal footing

We wanted children in Bamenda on equal footing with children elsewhere in the country. Robotics and AI classes were a way to give them that edge, and the classes began in {{facts.cameroonStarted}}, under Glappy Foundation.

Circuit workshop, Bamenda.

02

The holiday program

The holiday program is how the work grew. Every holiday the doors open, more children come into the school, and they train in AI and robotics. Nearly {{facts.cameroonTrained}} children have trained in holiday programs, and the number grows with each holiday.

Classrooms and learners building

In class, students work at laptops. They share a computer, write code, and build a robot on the table.
