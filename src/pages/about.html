About AfroBot

Giving every child the chance to build the future.

We give children with limited access the tools, mentors and confidence to build the future with robotics and AI.

AfroBot is a kids-only youth robotics and AI program of Glappy Foundation, a Texas nonprofit corporation. Glappy Foundation is separate from Glappy Inc. Children take part through a parent or guardian.

The work started in Cameroon in {{facts.cameroonStarted}} and has run for about {{facts.cameroonYears}} years. A pilot in McKinney, Texas, followed in 2024.

Access for every child

Robotics and AI should not depend on a family’s income or zip code. Team fees stay low, and sponsors and donors cover the rest. Kits and mentors are provided through partner schools. The focus is communities with fewer STEM opportunities in the US and Africa, and girls and boys are equally welcome.
