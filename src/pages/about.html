About

A kids-only program for the chance to build.

AfroBot is a kids-only youth robotics and AI program. It exists so more young people can move from curiosity to making, and from making toward a real next step.

The work is hands-on: robotics, AI, and engineering, in communities where those tools have been hard to reach. Registration for programs is not open yet. Dates and fees on older versions of this site were proposals. They are not a current offer.

AfroBot does not publish partner logos, children’s profiles, or results. It does not guarantee admission, scholarships, or a place on a competition team.

What “kids-only” means here

Children take part through a parent or guardian. Mentors and volunteers are screened before they work with students. We do not create a public page for a child.

Values

AfroBot’s first value, Ubuntu, comes from the founders’ roots in the African diaspora. The welcome is wider than that root: the program is for young people who have been left out of tech.

  1. UbuntuWe rise together. “I am because we are.”
  2. AskCuriosity before answers.
  3. BuildMake it real, then test it.
  4. ServeSolve for real people.
  5. WelcomeEveryone has a seat.
  6. CelebrateJoy is part of the work.
