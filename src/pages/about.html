About AfroBot

Why AfroBot exists

AfroBot is a platform for the African dream. Hands-on AI and robotics for young people with limited access.

Get involvedThe record so far

The platform

Students learn at their own pace. They build and test their ideas, then use what they learn on problems they know firsthand.

Africans and friends of Africa can help give more young people the tools, mentorship, and opportunities to build for their communities.

Why access matters

A young person’s chance to learn AI and robotics should never depend on where they’re born, or on what their school can afford.

When students build something themselves, they learn to ask better questions, test an idea, and improve it when it fails.

We want more young people to have that experience, and to turn it into opportunities for themselves and their communities.

Vision and mission

What we are here to do

Vision. Young people across Africa have the skills and opportunity to shape their communities’ future through AI and robotics.

Mission. Make hands-on AI and robotics education accessible where access is limited. Equip local coaches to teach it. Help students turn what they learn into solutions for their communities.

How students take part

Learn, build, get support, and share

  1. 1

    Learn

    Self-paced Moodle lessons are planned for students and instructors, including schools with limited equipment.

  2. 2

    Build

    In Workbench, students will practice on exercises such as a tabletop sorter, then apply that practice to a problem they choose. Illustrative example.

  3. 3

    Get support

    Local coaches help students test ideas, learn from mistakes, and improve their projects.

  4. 4

    Share

    The competition will be a place to show what they built, whom it helps, and what changed after testing.

Where this builds from

A newer initiative

This work builds on more than three years of student learning in Bamenda through Glappy Foundation, operating there as Glappy Village. AfroBot is the newer initiative that builds on that experience and brings young people together through robotics and AI.

The foundation

A program of Glappy Foundation

AfroBot is a kids-only youth robotics and AI program of Glappy Foundation, a Texas nonprofit corporation. Glappy Foundation is separate from Glappy Inc. Children take part through a parent or guardian.

Access for every child

Robotics and AI should not depend on a family’s income or zip code. Team fees stay low, and sponsors and donors cover the rest. Kits and mentors are provided through partner schools. The focus is communities with fewer STEM opportunities in North America and Africa, and girls and boys are equally welcome.
