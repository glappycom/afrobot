A kids-only program for the chance to build.

AfroBot is a kids-only youth robotics and AI program.

AfroBot is a program of Glappy Foundation, a 501(c)(3) nonprofit organization.

Children take part through a parent or guardian.

Access for every child

Robotics and AI should not depend on a family’s income or zip code. Team fees stay low, and sponsors and donors cover the rest. Kits and mentors are provided through partner schools. The focus is communities with fewer STEM opportunities in the US and Africa, and girls and boys are equally welcome.
