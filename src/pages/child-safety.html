Child safety

Children take part through adults we can account for.

Children take part through a parent or guardian.

Taking part

A parent or guardian signs a child up. If you’re under 18, ask an adult to use the form. Please don’t send a birth date, a school ID, or a photo of a child. We don’t put a child on a public page.

Screening

Adults who work with children apply, share references, and pass a background check when the law or the host site asks for one. They finish safety training before they work with youth.

Supervision

We follow a two-adult rule. No adult is alone with a child. Each event has an adult-to-child ratio for the ages and the activity, and families hear that ratio before the day. Children are signed in and signed out. Only an authorized adult may pick a child up. A parent or guardian is included on any message with a child.

Photo and media consent

We ask a parent or guardian for written consent before we photograph or film a child. We don’t publish children’s names. A family can opt out, and we honor that. Photos are stored securely. Ask us to take a photo down, and we will.

Reporting concerns

Email info@afrobot.org with the subject “Child safety”. Tell us what happened, when, and how to reach you. Leave out details we don’t need. Adults who work with AfroBot follow the reporting laws where the program takes place.

Health and safety at events

Before an event, we ask a parent or guardian for emergency contacts and for health notes the day needs, such as an allergy. A first-aid kit is on site, and we follow the venue’s emergency plan. We can give first aid. We don’t provide other medical care.
