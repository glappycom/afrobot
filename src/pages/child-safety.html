Child safety

Children take part through adults we can account for.

Children take part through a parent or guardian. Mentors are screened before they work with students.

Who submits information

A parent or guardian submits interest for a student. If you are under 18, ask an adult to use the form. Do not send a full date of birth, a school ID, or a photo of a child through the form.

No public child profile

AfroBot does not publish a page for a child. Photographs from 2024 do not name the children in them.

Mentors and volunteers

Screening is required before a person mentors, coaches, or volunteers with students.

Concerns

Email info@afrobot.org with the subject “Child safety”. Write what happened, when, and how to reach you. Do not include a child’s unnecessary personal details.
