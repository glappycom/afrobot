Child safety

Children take part through adults we can account for.

Children take part through a parent or guardian.

Taking part

A parent or guardian submits interest for a student. If you are under 18, ask an adult to use the form. Do not send a full date of birth, a school ID, or a photo of a child through the form. AfroBot does not publish a page for a child.

Screening

Adults who work with children complete an application, provide references, and pass a background check where the law or the host site requires one. They finish safety training before they work with youth. This is AfroBot’s own commitment. It is not a certification.

Supervision

AfroBot follows a two-adult rule. No adult is alone one-on-one with a child. Each event keeps an adult-to-child ratio set for the ages and the activity, and families are told that ratio before the day. Children are signed in and signed out. Only an authorized adult may pick a child up. Messages with a child include a parent or guardian on the thread.

Photo and media consent

AfroBot asks for written consent from a parent or guardian before photos or video of a child. Children’s names are not published. A family may opt out, and that choice is honored. Photos are stored securely. A parent or guardian may ask for a photo to be removed, and AfroBot will remove it.

Reporting concerns

To raise a concern, email info@afrobot.org with the subject “Child safety”. Write what happened, when, and how to reach you. Leave out personal details that are not needed. Adults who work with AfroBot follow mandatory reporting under the law where the program takes place.

Health and safety at events

Before an event, AfroBot asks the parent or guardian for emergency contacts and for health notes the day requires, such as an allergy. A first-aid kit is on site. The team follows the venue’s emergency plan. AfroBot does not provide medical care beyond first aid.
