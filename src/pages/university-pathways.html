University Pathways

Further learning, without a promise of admission.

Advance is for older students, suggested ages 15–18, who want making to connect to what they might study next. AfroBot does not guarantee college admission, scholarships, credit, or a campus visit.

A pathway, when one exists, should be practical: help naming skills a student practiced, an introduction a school asks for, or a workshop hosted with a class. None of those are scheduled on this site.

No universities are named here. A conversation with a school or a university starts as an inquiry. It does not put a logo on AfroBot, and it is not an application for admission.
