Partnership inquiries

Ask about support. Nothing is charged here.

Sponsors, schools, universities, and manufacturers can write. This page takes an inquiry. It does not take a payment, and it does not list you as a public partner.

A sponsorship inquiry is a question about covering a student’s place, equipment, or a future program. It is not a receipt. Tax treatment of a gift is not stated on this website.

If you hoped to see a partner list, the Partners page explains why it is empty.
