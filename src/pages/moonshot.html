Our moonshot

What if ten times as many kids could learn this?

What if we could teach 10 times more young people to build robots and use AI, without needing 10 times the teachers, rooms, or money?

This is a hope. We have not done it.

What we have done

Programs in Cameroon since {{facts.cameroonStarted}}, about {{facts.cameroonYears}} years. About {{facts.cameroonTrained}} children trained in holiday programs. About {{facts.cameroonActive}} learning now. A McKinney pilot of about {{facts.mckinneyStudents}} students in 2024.

What we are building

Academy, League, and Innovation Lab are the three programs. We plan a US season for 2027 and 2028. The Dallas date in April 2028 is proposed. The Global Innovation Challenge has no date yet.

The hope

Teach many more kids without hiring ten times the teachers or renting ten times the rooms. Ideas we may try:

  • A tutor that uses AI
  • Kits that pack small
  • Labs that schools share
  • The learning site
  • The same lessons in every city
  • Training the adults who teach
  • Partners who cover costs
  • Teams in Cameroon and the US working together

What we still need to measure

How many kids show up, how many come back, what they can build, and what it costs per child. We do not have that study yet.
