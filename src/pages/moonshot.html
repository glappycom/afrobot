Our moonshot

What if we could reach ten times as many young people?

What if we could enable 10 times more young people to learn, build, and innovate with advanced robotics and AI—without requiring 10 times the teachers, facilities, or operating resources?

This is an ambition. It is not a result we claim.

What we have achieved

Programs in Cameroon since {{facts.cameroonStarted}}, about {{facts.cameroonYears}} years. About {{facts.cameroonTrained}} children trained in holiday programs. About {{facts.cameroonActive}} learning now. A McKinney pilot of about {{facts.mckinneyStudents}} students in 2024.

What we are building

Academy, League, and Innovation Lab are the program shape. The US 2027–2028 season is proposed. The Global Innovation Challenge is proposed and has no date.

Our moonshot ambition

Serve many more students without a matching rise in teachers, rooms, and operating cost. The levers we are willing to test:

  • AI tutoring
  • Modular kits
  • Shared labs
  • The learning site
  • Standard curricula
  • Train-the-trainer
  • Partnerships
  • International collaboration

Evidence we plan to collect

Attendance, return rate, skills shown in a build, and cost per student. None of that evidence is published as a finished study.
