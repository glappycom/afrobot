What we are building

Academy, League, and Innovation Lab are the three programs. The 2027 season in Dallas–Fort Worth scales the 2024 pilot to about {{impact.dallas2027Teams}} teams and about {{impact.dallas2027Students}} students. A Dallas championship in April 2028 is proposed, and the Global Innovation Challenge has no date yet.

The hope

Teach many more kids without hiring ten times the teachers or renting ten times the rooms. Ideas we may try:

  • A tutor that uses AI
  • Kits that pack small
  • Labs that schools share
  • The learning site
  • The same lessons in every city
  • Training the adults who teach
  • Partners who cover costs
  • Teams in Africa and North America working together
