Accessibility

The target is WCAG 2.2 AA.

Pages are built to be used with a keyboard, with visible focus, with text that meets contrast on the ivory background, and with motion that stays still when the system asks for reduced motion.

What you should find

  • A skip link on every page.
  • One main heading, and section headings under it.
  • Form errors next to the field, also summarized in a status message.
  • The 2024 build-day video starts only if you press play. A short description sits beside it.

Known gaps

This is not a finished audit. The build-day video does not yet have a full audio description. If a page blocks you, email info@afrobot.org with the page address and what you were trying to do. We will reply.
