Accessibility

The target is WCAG 2.2 AA.

Pages are built for a keyboard, visible focus, contrast on white, and reduced motion when the system asks for it.

What you should find

  • A skip link on every page.
  • One main heading, and section headings under it.
  • Form errors next to the field, and in a status message.
  • A short description beside the 2024 build-day video.

Known gaps

The build-day video does not yet have a full audio description. If a page blocks you, email info@afrobot.org with the page address and what you were trying to do.
