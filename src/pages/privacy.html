Privacy

What the interest form collects.

This notice describes the form on this website. A full privacy policy is still in review.

Who should send it

The form is for adults. If you are under 18, ask a parent or guardian to submit it. AfroBot does not create a public profile for a child.

Fields

  • Name, email, role, and city and state are required.
  • Organization, a short note about children’s ages, and a message are optional.
  • You must agree that we may email you about AfroBot.

Do not include a child’s full date of birth, a school ID, financial documents, or account numbers.

What we do with it

Submissions are stored privately so the AfroBot team can reply. They are not shown on this website. We do not sell this information.

Videos

YouTube loads only when a visitor plays a video.

How long we keep it

We keep inquiries so we can answer them. A retention schedule is part of the policy still under review. To ask about a submission, email info@afrobot.org.
