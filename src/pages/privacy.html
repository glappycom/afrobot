What the form collects

Who should send it

Adults send the form. If you’re under 18, ask a parent or guardian. We don’t make a public profile for a child.

Fields

Name, email, role, and city and state are required. Organization, a short note about children’s ages, and a message are optional. You must agree that we may email you about AfroBot. A school sign-up also includes the school, its location, an optional phone number, and what the school is asking for. Please don’t include a child’s full birth date, a school ID, financial documents, or account numbers.

What we do with it

We keep what you send so we can write back. It stays off this website, and we don’t sell it.

How long we keep it

We keep inquiries so we can answer them. Email hello@afrobot.org if you want to ask about yours.
