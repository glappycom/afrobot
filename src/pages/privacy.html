Privacy

What the interest form collects.

This notice describes the form on this website. It is not a finished legal policy.

Content pending. A full privacy policy is waiting on legal review. Until then, the notes below match how the form works today.

Who should send it

The form is for adults: a parent or guardian, an educator, a mentor, a sponsor, or someone writing for an organization. If you are under 18, ask a parent or guardian to submit it. AfroBot does not create a public profile for a child.

Fields

  • Name, email, role, and city and state are required.
  • Organization, a short note about children’s ages, and a message are optional.
  • You must agree that we may email you about AfroBot.

Please do not include a child’s full date of birth, a school ID, financial documents, or account numbers. The financial-assistance question does not ask for them.

What we do with it

Submissions are stored privately so the AfroBot team can reply. They are not shown on this website. We do not sell this information. A hidden field is used to drop spam, and messages sent too quickly are dropped the same way.

How long we keep it

We keep inquiries so we can answer them. A retention schedule is part of the policy still under review. To ask about a submission, email info@afrobot.org.
