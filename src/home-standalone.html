Youth robotics & AI · Africa and North America

AfroBot is a platform for the African dream.

We’re opening up practical AI and robotics education for young people in Africa and other communities where access is limited. Students learn at their own pace, build and test their ideas, and use what they learn to tackle problems they know firsthand. We invite Africans and friends of Africa to help give more young people the tools, mentorship, and opportunities to build solutions for their communities.

Why AfroBot

A chance to learn should not depend on where a young person is born.

A young person’s chance to learn AI and robotics should never depend on where they’re born or what their school can afford. When students get to build something themselves, they learn to ask better questions, test an idea, and improve it when it fails. We want more young people to have that experience and turn those skills into opportunities for themselves and their communities.

Illustrative example

From a problem they notice to a solution they improve

This example is illustrative. It is not a story of a particular student.

  1. Notice

    A problem close to home

    A student notices a chore in their community that people still do by hand.

  2. Test

    Practice on a tabletop sorter

    In AfroBot Workbench, students will practice by training an image model for a tabletop sorter, then test what it gets wrong.

  3. Improve

    Try again, then choose a problem

    They improve the attempt, and use that same habit on a problem they choose.
